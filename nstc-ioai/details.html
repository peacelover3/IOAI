📋 Contest Rules & Guidelines

Everything you need to know to participate in NSTC contests

General Rules

Welcome to the NSTC (National Science & Technology Championship) contests! These are prestigious competitions designed to showcase talent, innovation, and problem-solving skills in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Technology. Please read these rules carefully before participating.

Eligibility

  • All students from Pakistan are eligible to participate
  • No prior experience required
  • Individual or team participation (details in contest-specific rules)
  • Must have valid contact information

Registration & Participation

  • Register through the official WhatsApp community channel
  • Complete all required information accurately
  • One registration per participant
  • Confirm participation by the announced deadline

Contest Conduct

  • Contests are held online at announced times
  • Follow all contest instructions strictly
  • No external resources during the contest (unless specified)
  • Maintain academic integrity at all times
  • Respect time limits for all challenges

Evaluation & Scoring

  • Solutions are evaluated on correctness and efficiency
  • Scoring details vary by contest type
  • Final rankings posted within 7 days of contest conclusion
  • Appeals can be filed within 48 hours of results announcement

Code of Conduct

  • Maintain respectful behavior towards all participants
  • No plagiarism or copying of solutions
  • Report any technical issues immediately
  • No sharing of contest questions or solutions before results are announced

Disqualification

Participants may be disqualified for:

  • Cheating or plagiarism
  • Abusive behavior or harassment
  • Technical violations or hacking attempts
  • Repeated rule violations

Note: For detailed contest-specific rules, see the complete document below.

📚 Comprehensive Syllabus

Topics and learning objectives for NSTC competitions

Introduction to AI & Machine Learning

This section covers the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning concepts that form the foundation of modern technology competitions.

Core Topics

  • AI Fundamentals: Definition, types, and applications of AI
  • Machine Learning Basics: Supervised vs Unsupervised Learning
  • Neural Networks: Perceptrons, Deep Learning architectures
  • Natural Language Processing: Text processing and analysis
  • Computer Vision: Image processing and recognition

Programming & Problem Solving

Develop strong programming fundamentals and algorithmic thinking.

Key Skills

  • Python Programming: Variables, loops, functions, OOP
  • Data Structures: Arrays, Linked Lists, Trees, Graphs
  • Algorithms: Sorting, Searching, Dynamic Programming
  • Complexity Analysis: Big O notation and optimization
  • Debugging & Testing: Code quality and verification

Data Science & Analytics

Master data manipulation and analytical techniques.

Topics Covered

  • Data Analysis: Pandas, NumPy, data cleaning
  • Statistics: Probability, distributions, hypothesis testing
  • Visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn, interactive charts
  • Big Data: Handling large datasets efficiently

Competitive Programming

Advanced techniques for solving competition-level problems.

Advanced Topics

  • Graph Algorithms: BFS, DFS, Shortest Path, Network Flow
  • Advanced DP: Multi-dimensional DP, Bitmask DP
  • Greedy Algorithms: Optimization and correctness proofs
  • Mathematical Concepts: Number theory, Combinatorics

Real-World Applications

Learn how AI and ML are applied in industry and research.

Practical Areas

  • Recommendation Systems
  • Autonomous Systems
  • Healthcare Analytics
  • Financial Technology
  • Smart Cities & IoT

Tip: Focus on understanding concepts deeply rather than memorizing. Practice coding regularly and solve diverse problems.
